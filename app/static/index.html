      Add your provider's XMLTV address under Source. After the first sync every channel is listed here in upstream order, ready to drag into place. Channels that appear later are added to the end.

      Viewers

      Each viewer gets a guide address of their own. Paste it into their player as the TV guide (EPG) address. Revoking a viewer cuts off that address only.

      Addresses end in .xml.gz because some players need that ending to unpack the guide. For plain XML swap it for .xml; it still travels compressed when the player allows. /epg.xml.gz?key=KEY works as well.
