Groups

Players show groups in this order. Drag a group to move it, or drop a channel on one. A tick turns a whole group on or off.

    To move a channel, drag its grip into place or onto a group, type a new number in its box, or focus the grip and press the arrow keys.

      No channels yet

      Add your provider's playlist address under Source, then press Sync now. Every channel is listed here in the provider's own groups, ready to drag into place. Channels that appear later join the end of their group.

      Viewers

      Each viewer gets addresses of their own. With a playlist published, add the playlist address in the player as an M3U playlist: it names that viewer's guide, so the TV guide should follow on its own. If a player does not pick it up, add the guide address too. Revoking a viewer cuts off that viewer only.

      The playlist hands a viewer your provider's stream addresses, login included, so give it only to people you would give that login to. Guide addresses end in .xml.gz because some players need that ending; swap it for .xml for plain XML. ?key=KEY on /playlist.m3u or /epg.xml.gz works as well.
